Privacy Policy

Application VOIDWAKE (package ru.voidwake.game)
Effective date: October 10, 2026
Русская версия (the Russian version prevails in case of discrepancies)

1. General provisions

This Policy describes what data the VOIDWAKE mobile application processes when distributed through RuStore and other channels. By using the application, you agree to the terms of this Policy.

2. What data is processed

Starting with version 1.3.0, the application:

  • stores on the device a local high-score table, the pilot name and game progress — crystals, upgrades, ships and cosmetics, daily missions, season pass progress, rewarded-ad bonus limits and the state of special offers (via WebView storage / localStorage);
  • uses the AppMetrica analytics service (YANDEX LLC) for usage statistics and crash reports: technical device and application identifiers (the advertising ID is not available — the AD_ID permission is excluded), device model, OS version, language, approximate region based on IP, game events (run start and end, wave reached, purchases in the in-game hangar for in-game currency, ad views, start and result of an in-app purchase without payment details) and crash diagnostics. Precise location is not collected. Data is processed in anonymized form in accordance with the AppMetrica terms of use;
  • shows ads through the Yandex Advertising Network (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK, YANDEX LLC): rewarded videos (“second chance”, “×2 loot”, supply capsule and other bonuses) — only when the player taps the button, and full-screen ads between runs (not in the first runs; turned off by the “No ads” purchase). The SDK receives technical device and application data (model, OS version, language, network type, IP address and the approximate region derived from it, impression and click events). Ads are requested in child-directed (age-restricted) mode without personalization: no personalization consent is passed, location is not used, and the advertising ID permission (AD_ID) is removed from the application, so the Google advertising ID is not used for advertising purposes. More details — Yandex Mobile Ads SDK terms;
  • offers optional in-app purchases (crystals, bundles, ad removal, the premium track of the season pass, special offers); payment is processed through RuStore Pay (VK LLC): card data and payment details are entered in the RuStore payment window and are not shared with the developer. The application receives only the purchase and product identifiers, the payment status and the test-payment flag, and stores the list of granted purchases on the device so that they are not credited twice. The RuStore SDK also sends technical metrics of its own operation to RuStore;
  • may store sound settings and internal game parameters on the device; the progress backup code is generated on the device when the player taps the button and is not sent anywhere;
  • in the browser version on the Yandex Games platform, progress may be synchronized via Yandex Games cloud saves, and ads are shown by the platform — under the Yandex Games rules;
  • does not require registration or signing in to an account;
  • does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone or location;
  • when the online leaderboard is enabled (optional), may send the pilot name, score and wave number to the developer’s server for the high-score table; if the URL is not configured, the data stays on the device only;

3. Android permissions

The application uses the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions — for AppMetrica analytics, loading ads, RuStore payments and (optionally) online high scores, VIBRATE — for in-game vibration (can be turned off in the settings), and RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — added by the RuStore SDK to deliver its technical metrics. The com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission is intentionally removed from the build. Gameplay and progress do not depend on the network: without internet access, ads are simply not shown and purchases are unavailable.

4. Transfer to third parties

The data listed in section 2 is transferred to YANDEX LLC as the operator of the AppMetrica services (statistics and diagnostics) and the Yandex Advertising Network (selection and display of non-personalized ads, impression accounting), and, for purchases, to the RuStore operator (VK LLC) to process the payment. Data is not transferred to or sold to any other third parties. When the application is installed from RuStore, the rules and policy of the RuStore store also apply.

5. Storage and deletion

Local data is stored only on the user’s device. Uninstalling the application or clearing the application data in Android settings deletes the saved high scores, progress and settings. Analytics data is stored in AppMetrica for the periods set by the service terms.

6. Children

The application is not intended to collect data from children. The recommended age rating is specified in the RuStore listing (guideline: 6+ / arcade game without harmful content). Therefore, ads are requested with the child-directed flag and without personalization, and the advertising ID is not used.

7. Changes to this Policy

In case of material changes (for example, connecting new services or collecting new types of data), this Policy will be updated and its effective date changed. The current version is published at this address.

8. Contact

For questions about data processing and the publication of VOIDWAKE, contact the developer using the contact details provided in the application’s listing in RuStore.
